1. Write quick, simple prompts and see how it does.
2.  Based on where the output falls short, flesh out the prompt iteratively. This often leads to a longer, more detailed, prompt, perhaps even a mega-prompt.
3. If that’s still insufficient, consider few-shot or many-shot learning (if applicable) or, less frequently, fine-tuning.
4. If that still doesn’t yield the results you need, break down the task into subtasks and apply an agentic workflow.

A deeper dive into prompting strategies, I recommend the Medprompt [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.16452) , which lays out a complex set of prompting strategies that can lead to very good results.
