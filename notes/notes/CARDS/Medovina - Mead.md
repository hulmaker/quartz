# Bochet
**Bochet** is a type of mead made from honey that has been caramelized. Black color.

https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bochet
https://youtu.be/nTRwIf2apWc?si=p596dvTqNBmTPkTt

> [!WARNING]
> NEVER add water to boiling honey. The water will instantly turn to steam and cause a "steam explosion", splattering boiling hot honey everywhere. The correct technique is to let the honey cool to just under 100 C (say to about 90 C) and only then add the water.
