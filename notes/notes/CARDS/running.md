pyramid:
* based on time, rather than distance
* 15-20 mins warmup,
* 7 mins @ HM effort, 2 min jog,
* 10 mins @ HM effort, 2 min jog,
* 14 mins @ HM effort, 2 min jog,
* 10 mins @ HM effort, 2 min jog,
* 7 mins @ HM effort,
* 15-20 mins cool down
* totals 48 mins @ HM effort.

fartlek:
* warmup,
* 1m @ 5K-10K effort, 1m jog,
* repeat previous step 10-12x,
* cool down

tempo:
* warmup
* 3.2K @ tempo interval effort, 4 min jog,
* repeat previous step 2-3x,
* cooldown

cruise:
 * warmup,
 * 1K @ cruise interval effort, 2 min jog,
 * repeat previous step 6-8x,
 * cool down
