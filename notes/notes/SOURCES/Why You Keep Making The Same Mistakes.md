## Necessary 2 processes for corrective action
People usually have only one of these two processes. But you need both.
1. **Counter-factual thinking**: what went wrong, how to get there, where I am
2. **Motivational component**: A will to solve it

### Motivational fuel to do corrective action
**Bad**: shame, guilt, pain, hunger ...
* shame -> malice, resentment, avoidance
* shame works **only** if there is no other choice to do it, otherwise shame leads to [[Avoidance]]
* Stop avoiding the shame -> then it can work as a fuel

### Counter factual thinking
* what should have I done differently
* If you live [[Avoidance]] life, your brain gets <mark style="background: #FF5582A6;">worse</mark> at counter factual thinking - less data, worse acc
	* retrospective data is less valuable than prospective data
* Avoidance instead of counter factual thinking - doing other things instead of evaluating

## Ask these questions
- Am I just theory crafting without doing anything?
- What is the percentage of theory crafting vs data collection?
- When you feel shame, do you harness it to make change in your life, or are you avoiding the shame and progressing forward?
