# Mild peripheral-only symptoms
* runny nose, minor sore throat, but you feel **STRONG** (NO fever)
* It's ok to do either two things: Both ok, keep you from getting **MORE** sick
	1. **Less volume** - same loads and exercises, but reduce sets to 2/3 of the orig. plan
	2. **Recovery session** - 50% load, 50% sets until you're 100% ok
	
# Gnarly peripheral-only symptoms
* major runny nose, super sore throat, but you feel **OK** in your muscles (NO fever)
* It's ok to do either two things: Pushing hard makes you **MORE** sick.
	1. **Recovery session** - 50% load, 50% sets until you're 100% ok
	2. **Time away** - sleep, eat, drink, relax extra (probably better - don't spread germs)

# Central symptoms
* fever, fatigue, body aches, etc.
* Just stay home, sleep, eat, drink, relax extra... Pushing it hard will probably get you MUCH MORE sick.
