> Embrace the suck. There will always be a friction.
# What is it
Anterior Mid-Cingulate Cortex is
- A brain area that is related to willpower.
- Scientists think it is the seat of willpower, or even
- The seat of will to LIVE

# What it does
**Anterior Mid-Cingulate Cortex**
- Do something you don't want to do - it grows
- Do only things you want to do - it shrinks
- If you don't like cold water, it grows, if you happen to like it the next day, it doesn't. If you don't force yourself the next day, it shrinks.
- Fat people - small, gets bigger when they diet
- Athletes - large, long living people - large

# Why it matters
* the brain area behaves in a way that human will does, there are direct connections
* To have a will to live, you should do something you hate every day
* There is a cure for alcoholism, but it only works for one day. Then next day, you have to start again

** How this relates to [[Burnout]]?**
> [[Burnout]] is an extreme effort with a lack of reward.

This concept encourages you to put in an effort in something you don't want to do. But there should be always a reward. Otherwise it can lead to burnout.
