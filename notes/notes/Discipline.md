> If you are here because you have trouble sustaining the effort. **Please checkout**: [[The Anti-Exhaustion Principle - The Path to Loving the Grind]]


- **Circuit of value**: Your brain constantly does cost/benefit analysis. What's the point of doing things if you already have what you want and it's cheap?
- **Temporal bias and future discounting**: You value instant gratification over long term value.
- **Competing interest**: you need something that is worth to "pay the price" of discipline. What is worth the pain for you.
- Can you distinguish between your wants and what other thinks you should do?
- You need to be ready to change or at least be wishing to be ready to change:
- List of things you "should do" -> Pick what you want and what others want
- Plan steps you can take to achieve just one thing. Make the step small enough so you can pass it every day. See [[Consistency]]
- Be vary of the pitfall of self doubt. "It's not enough" and "100% or nothing" thoughts are a trap.
- Keep doing that, monitor internal dialog. The future is built and it takes time.
