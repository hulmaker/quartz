Good startup metric (from investors):
- Ability to expand across border (foreign investment)
- defensibility of the technology (difficult to do what they do)
- focus on customer. You do the thing primary for them 

Startup common mistakes:
- no ai start-ups in future, maybe wrappers
- chatgpt wrapper does not add value 
- ability to adapt
- really think about the value proposition
- the value has to be solid and inherent, otherwise it will fail eventually
