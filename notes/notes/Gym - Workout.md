## Basic Strength Workout (Gym with machines)
[source](https://www.puregym.com/blog/gym-workout-plans-for-men/)
**Day 1 - Push**
- Dumbbell bench press -- 4 sets | reps: 6-8, 10-12, 10-12, 12+
- Chest press machine -- 3 sets | reps: 6-8, 8-10, 10-12
- Cable flys – 2 sets | reps: 12-15
- Shoulder press machine -- 3 sets | reps: 10-21
- Tricep push down -- 3 sets | reps: 12
**Day 2 - Pull**
- Lat pulldown -- 4 sets | reps: 6-8, 10-12, 10-12, 12+
- Bent over rows -- 3 sets | reps: 6-8, 10-12, 10-12
- Chest supported row -- 3 sets | reps: 6-8, 10-12, 12+
- Face pulls -- 3 sets | reps: 12-15
- Bicep curls -- 1 sets | reps: 7 half reps (bottom), 7 half reps (top), 7 full reps
**Day 3 - Legs**
- Dumbbell squats -- 3 sets | reps: 10-12
- Leg press -- 4 sets | reps: 6-8, 10-12, 10-12, 12-15
- Hamstring curl -- 3 sets | reps: 12-15
- Walking lunges --2 sets | reps: 12 steps each side
- Calf raises -- 3 sets | reps: 25-30

## Joe Rogan Kettlebell Workout
[short](https://youtube.com/shorts/Xdz99XJyU_Y?si=bw_p-cKBbjwMsqjf), [full](https://youtu.be/kJWuRmHEjk4?si=NLpNxYV366DPKvaM), [Dr.Mike reaction](https://youtu.be/LB7WXv7uUsg?si=atPdUJkkObBAjgaq)
**warm up**: -- 2-5 sets
- 20 bodyweight squats (optionally elevate heels)
- 20 pushups
**workout**:
- one arm swings -- 3 sets, 10 each arm
- clean & press -- 3 sets, 10 each arm (bonus: squat after press)
- windmill -- 3 sets, 10 each arm (30% lighter ktb)
- push up + renegade row -- 3 sets, 10 each arm
**Notes**: Focus on range of motion, stretch, hamstrings, lower back...
