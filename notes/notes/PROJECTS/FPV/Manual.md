# SD Card
You will need a decent SD card for [Blackbox logging](https://oscarliang.com/blackbox/), [see my SD card recommendations](https://oscarliang.com/sd-cards-fpv/#blackbox). Betaflight firmware requires the type of the microSD card to be either Standard (SDSC) or High Capacity (SDHC), so extended capacity cards (SDXC) are not supported (Many high-speed U3 cards are SDXC). Also, the card MUST be formatted in FAT32 filesystems. So basically, you could use any SD card smaller than 32GB, but Betaflight can only recognize 4GB maximum, any additional storage would be wasted.
