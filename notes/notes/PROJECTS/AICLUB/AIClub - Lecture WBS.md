# Work Breakdown Structure
Clockify tasks:
AIC02S1 - Initial Research, Content Development, Slide Creation

# Simplified WBS
#### Stage 1: Content Development
* Topic Selection and Initial Research
* Planning and Structuring - objectives, outline, break down content
* Content - key ideas, materials, visual aids
#### Stage 2: Slide Creation
* content into slides, add interesting visuals
#### Stage 3:  Rehearsal and Feedback
* practice, time
* gather feedback, adjustments
* prepare notes, backup materials, final preparations
#### Stage 4: Presentation, Post-Presentation
* Presentation day, feedback, reflect

# Detailed WBS
### Stage 1: Topic Selection and Research

**1.1 Choose a Topic**
- Select an engaging and relevant subject.

**1.2 Conduct Initial Research**
- Gather basic information and identify key points.

**1.3 Identify Target Audience**
- Determine who the lecture is aimed at and their needs.

### Stage 2: Planning and Structuring

**2.1 Define Objectives**
- Establish the main goals of the presentation.

**2.2 Create an Outline**
- Develop a detailed outline of the lecture.

**2.3 Break Down Content**
- Divide the outline into manageable sections.

### Stage 3: Content Development

**3.1 Develop Key Points**
- Expand on main topics with detailed information.

**3.2 Gather Supporting Materials**
- Collect data, statistics, quotes, and other supporting content.

**3.3 Create Visual Aids**
- Design charts, graphs, and images to illustrate key points.

### Stage 4: Slide Creation

**4.1 Choose Presentation Software**
- Select appropriate software (e.g., PowerPoint, Keynote).

**4.2 Design Slide Templates**
- Create a consistent and visually appealing slide design.

**4.3 Populate Slides with Content**
- Insert text, images, and visuals into slides.

**4.4 Review and Edit Slides**
- Check for clarity, consistency, and errors.

### Stage 5: Rehearsal and Feedback

**5.1 Practice Presentation**
- Rehearse the lecture multiple times.

**5.2 Time the Presentation**
- Ensure it fits within the allocated time.

**5.3 Gather Feedback**
- Present to a small group and collect their feedback.

**5.4 Make Necessary Adjustments**
- Refine content and delivery based on feedback.

### Stage 6: Final Preparations

**6.1 Prepare Notes**
- Create speaker notes and cue cards.

**6.2 Technical Setup**
- Check all technical equipment (projector, mic, etc.).

**6.3 Backup Materials**
- Have digital and physical backups of the presentation.

**6.4 Final Rehearsal**
- Conduct a final run-through.

### Stage 7: Presentation Day

**7.1 Arrive Early**
- Set up and test equipment at the venue.

**7.2 Engage Audience**
- Start with an engaging introduction.

**7.3 Deliver Presentation**
- Present confidently, maintaining eye contact and clear speech.

**7.4 Q&A Session**
- Prepare to answer audience questions effectively.

**7.5 Gather Feedback**
- Collect audience feedback post-presentation.

### Stage 8: Post-Presentation

**8.1 Review Feedback**
- Analyze the feedback for future improvements.

**8.2 Reflect on Performance**
- Identify strengths and areas for improvement.

**8.3 Archive Materials**
- Save and organize all materials for future reference.

**8.4 Plan Follow-Up**
- Schedule any necessary follow-up actions or communications.
