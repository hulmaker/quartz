## Discharge curve 
* Li-Po - linear discharge till 3.2V, then cliff fall 
* Li-Ion - linear discharge till 2.2V (in theory, 2.6V-2.7V ish in reality) - (the area after the LiPo fall gives LiIon the additional amps. )

## Full Throttle
* Li-Po - voltage falls from 4.0V to 3.6V and then quickly gets up to 4.0V
* Li-Ion - large voltage sag. voltage falls from 3.9V to 2.8V - then you have to add throttle sooner, cos of the fall. -  The drone is then much less explosive because of the battery.

## Conclusion
Li-Ion are **better for cruising**, but much **much worse for freestyle**, puch outs, throttle control etc. That is why dji drones use LI-Ion batteries. Then there are differences in temperature stability, life expectancy etc.
