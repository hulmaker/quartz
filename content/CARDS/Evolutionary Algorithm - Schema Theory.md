Subset of the [[Evolutionary Algorithm]]

**Definition**: A template, which defines set of solutions from the search space with certain specific similarities.
**Example**: $S = (* * 1 * 01 * 0 **)$, where * is a wildcard
**fitness**: average fitness computed over all covered strings
