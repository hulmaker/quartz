## Backpack
- Max 65 L.
- Max 1.36 kg

## Sleeping bag / Quilt
- At least 800 fill power
- Max 1.13 kg

## Footwear
- Buy shoes a size too big
- Avoid footwear with waterproof liners

## Shelter
- Lightweight - Max. 1.36 kg.
- Stormy worthy and Comfortable
- Third of the time is under the shelter

## Outer Layer
- A good durable water repellant
- Lightweit - under 10 oz
- Quick drying
- Pit zips for ventilation
- Adjustable wrist cuffs
- Adjustable hood with a stiff brim

## Other stuff
- Pillow
- Butt pad
- Camelback
- Trekking poles
- Fork-o-spoon / fork'n spoon
- Map and compass?
