#### DeepTech investing by Founder & VC fund Perspective
- Try to get grants - they are equity free
- what are VC looking for in a founder: identified the problem, inventions, ideas, intentions
- "red flag": not able to move where the customer is (40-50% want to stay home)
- you need diverse minds - not only technical people! (Go to customers, get feedback)
- selling vs overselling, selling bullshit (Elisabeth Holmes)
- great ideas can raise money. Tech startup need business person
- mindset for marathon, not sprint
- Mindset: I have to succeed and I have to figure out how 
- seed, series A, B - co to je za fáze?
- travel, meet founders, see what you are missing

#### Czech & Israel startup workshop
- It takes a village to raise a child = startup 
- must read: the lean startup
- book: startup nation - tel Aviv 
- follow Simon Sinek (yt) - high trust & high trust in themselves = successful ppl
- performance/trust chart (navy seals) - low perf, high trust is person who you want
- introduce yourself: (most people) what, how, why (few people)
- org with weak why dies (kodak)
